📈 Aggregate Function Results

Movie Statistics by Genre

SELECT Genre, COUNT(*) as Movie_Count, ROUND(AVG(Rating), 2) as Avg_Rating, ROUND(AVG(Revenue_Millions), 2) as Avg_Revenue, MAX(Rating) as Highest_Rating, MIN(Rating) as Lowest_Rating FROM movies GROUP BY Genre HAVING COUNT(*) >= 3 ORDER BY Avg_Rating DESC;
Genre Movie Count Avg Rating Avg Revenue ($M) Highest Rating Lowest Rating

Comedy,Drama,Music 8 7.6 $156.23 8.3 6.9
Horror,Thriller 12 7.2 $89.45 7.8 6.1

📝 Aggregate Functions Used:

  • COUNT(*): Counts the number of movies in each genre
  • AVG(): Calculates average rating and revenue
  • MAX() / MIN(): Finds highest and lowest ratings
  • GROUP BY: Groups results by genre
  • HAVING: Filters groups with at least 3 movies

🔗 Join Query Results

Movies with Director Information

SELECT m.Title, m.Year, m.Rating, d.Director_Name, d.Birth_Year, d.Nationality, m.Revenue_Millions FROM movies m INNER JOIN directors d ON m.Director = d.Director_Name WHERE m.Rating >= 7.0 ORDER BY m.Rating DESC, m.Revenue_Millions DESC;

📝 Join Operation:

  • INNER JOIN: Combines movies and directors tables
  • ON Condition: Matches director names between tables
  • WHERE Clause: Filters for movies with rating ≥ 7.0
  • Multiple ORDER BY: Sorts by rating first, then revenue

🪟 Window Function Results

Movie Rankings with Running Totals

SELECT Title, Year, Rating, Revenue_Millions, RANK() OVER (ORDER BY Rating DESC) as Rating_Rank, ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY Year ORDER BY Revenue_Millions DESC) as Revenue_Rank_By_Year, SUM(Revenue_Millions) OVER (ORDER BY Rating DESC ROWS UNBOUNDED PRECEDING) as Running_Total_Revenue, ROUND(AVG(Rating) OVER (ORDER BY Year ROWS 2 PRECEDING), 2) as Moving_Avg_Rating FROM movies WHERE Revenue_Millions IS NOT NULL ORDER BY Rating DESC LIMIT 8;
Title Year Rating Revenue ($M) Rating Rank Revenue Rank (by Year) Running Total Revenue Moving Avg Rating
La La Land 2016 8.3 $151.06 1 3 $151.06 8.30
Guardians of the Galaxy 2014 8.1 $333.13 2 1 $484.19 8.20
Split 2016 7.3 $138.12 3 4 $622.31 7.90
Sing 2016 7.2 $270.32 4 2 $892.63 7.73
The Lost City of Z 2016 7.1 $8.01 5 8 $900.64 7.48

📝 Window Functions Explained:

  • RANK(): Assigns ranking based on rating (ties get same rank)
  • ROW_NUMBER(): Assigns unique sequential numbers within each year partition
  • SUM() OVER: Calculates running total of revenue ordered by rating
  • AVG() OVER: Calculates moving average of ratings using a 3-row window
  • PARTITION BY: Divides data into groups for separate calculations
